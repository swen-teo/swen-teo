About me

I'm an Information Systems student at SMU who works at the intersection of software development, data analytics and digital engagement.

My coursework and projects cover the whole digital product lifecycle: designing and building user-centred applications, then applying statistical modelling and social data mining to understand how they're used. I turn complex datasets into insights with dashboards in Tableau and Power BI, and I care about interfaces that make those insights easy to act on.

Alongside my degree I teach: I'm a Teaching Assistant for Web Application Development II at SMU and at Kumon, and I run marketing for SMU Blockchain Club.
